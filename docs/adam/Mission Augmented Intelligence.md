# Mission Augmented Intelligence

ModifyMe exists to explore how emerging technologies can develop solutions that promote **augmented intelligence**.

## Beneficiaries

1. **High entrepreneurial intention (EI)** — people building products and ventures who need leverage from agents, knowledge systems, and generative interfaces.
2. **Cognitively impaired individuals** — people who benefit from assistive, clarifying, and scaffolding AI that reduces cognitive load without removing agency.

## How A.D.A.M. serves the mission

| Sphere                  | Contribution                                                                      |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agentic Development** | Build GenUI, agent runtimes, and product surfaces that make intelligence _usable_ |
| **Agent Management**    | Orchestrate many agents safely (worktrees, beads, context, ADRs) so R&D compounds |

## Design principles

- **Augment, don't replace** — keep human judgment in the loop for high-stakes decisions
- **Context as artifact** — durable docs (ADRs, beads, inbox knowledge) beat ephemeral chat
- **Accessible defaults** — clear language, progressive disclosure, recoverable failures
- **R&D capture** — every useful link or insight can enter [[Inbox Capture Protocol]] and become searchable knowledge
